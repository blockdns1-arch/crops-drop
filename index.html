Drop

A censorship-resistant, private, self-expiring message. Encrypted in your browser, stored on Arkiv, readable only by whoever holds the link, gone at expiry. No server, no account.

How it works
  • Your message is encrypted with AES-256-GCM in your browser before it leaves the page.
  • Only the ciphertext and a few indexed attributes are stored as an Arkiv entity. No plaintext, ever.
  • The decryption key lives in the # fragment of the link, which browsers never send to a server.
  • The entity expires automatically at the block you chose - nothing to delete, nothing to leak.

Built on Arkiv, the Web3 database. Track: Privacy (CROPS).
